UAE Golden Visa for Investors and Business Owners: What You Need to Know

The UAE Golden Visa has become one of the most discussed aspects of doing business in the Emirates. Since its introduction, it has been expanded and refined significantly - and for many international investors and entrepreneurs, it is now a realistic path to long-term residency that removes the friction of repeated visa renewals and sponsor dependency.

This article sets out the main routes, the practical requirements, and the implications for how you structure your business presence in the UAE. If you are still at the company formation stage, read our guide to UAE mainland vs free zone structures first.

What is the UAE Golden Visa?

The Golden Visa is a long-term UAE residency permit - issued for either 5 or 10 years and renewable - that does not require a UAE national sponsor and is not tied to employment with a specific company. Holders can sponsor their spouse, children and certain household staff under the same visa category.

Unlike standard residence visas linked to employment or company ownership, a Golden Visa allows you to remain resident in the UAE even if your company structure changes, your employment ends, or you spend extended periods abroad. The 6-month continuous absence rule that applies to standard visas does not apply to Golden Visa holders.

The Investor Route

The most commonly discussed Golden Visa category for business investors requires one of the following:

  • Real estate investment of AED 2 million or more in UAE property (fully paid, not mortgaged beyond the threshold). The property can be in a single title or across multiple properties reaching the combined threshold.
  • Investment in a UAE public fund or financial portfolio of AED 2 million or more, confirmed by the relevant authority.
  • Deposit of AED 2 million or more in a UAE bank for a minimum of 3 years.

Each route requires specific documentation and attestation from the relevant UAE authority - the property route requires a title deed and valuation certificate; the fund route requires confirmation from the fund manager and the UAE Securities and Commodities Authority.

The Entrepreneur and Business Owner Route

For founders and business operators, the entrepreneur category is the more relevant path. The current requirements include:

  • Ownership of an existing UAE company with a minimum annual turnover of AED 1 million, confirmed by an audited financial statement
  • Alternatively, approval from an accredited UAE business incubator for early-stage founders - though this route is more tightly controlled and sector-specific
  • A minimum share capital of AED 500,000 for certain business structures

The annual turnover requirement means this route is not immediately accessible to newly formed companies. For businesses in their first year of operations, the investor route via real estate or banking deposit is typically more achievable if long-term residency is a priority from the start.

The Skilled Professional Route

Senior executives, doctors, engineers, scientists and holders of certain professional qualifications may be eligible for a Golden Visa on a talent and specialisation basis, independent of investment thresholds. This route is managed through the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) and requires specific professional validation. It is worth noting for key staff members within your UAE operation.

What the Golden Visa Means for Business Structure

The choice of company structure and the Golden Visa are more connected than they might initially appear.

A mainland company with demonstrable annual revenue and audited accounts provides a clear pathway to the entrepreneur category Golden Visa over time. A free zone entity may qualify too, but the audit requirements and proof of UAE-origin revenue can be more complex to satisfy depending on the zone and the business model.

If long-term UAE residency is part of the overall plan - whether for personal tax residency, family settlement, or simply operational continuity - this should be factored into the structure conversation early. The company type, its licensing jurisdiction, its accounting and audit obligations, and its revenue profile all bear on Golden Visa eligibility. Banking and the Golden Visa

Golden Visa holders are generally viewed more favourably by UAE banks when applying for personal and business banking facilities. Residency stability is a positive indicator for banks assessing KYC compliance and long-term client relationships. If banking is a parallel priority, see our guide to UAE business banking requirements.

Key Practical Points

  • Applications are processed through the ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) and the relevant emirate's immigration authority - timelines vary
  • Entry permits, status adjustments and Emirates ID issuance are separate steps in the overall process
  • Requirements and thresholds can change - the figures in this article reflect the position as of early 2026
  • Some free zones have their own investor visa tracks separate from the federal Golden Visa programme - these are not equivalent and do not carry the same long-term residency benefits
