Our Services

A comprehensive suite of commercial brokerage and advisory services designed to help entrepreneurs, SMEs and international investors establish, structure and grow in the UAE and beyond.

Commercial Brokerage

We facilitate commercial transactions between buyers, sellers, investors and operators across a range of business types. Our role is to identify qualified counterparties, facilitate introductions and support negotiations - all with the discretion and professionalism the UAE market demands.

Company Formation - UAE & International

End-to-end support for setting up companies in the UAE mainland, free zones or internationally. We advise on the most appropriate structure for your activity and objectives, then coordinate the full formation process through our licensed partner network - from initial approvals through to licence issuance.

Corporate Structuring

Strategic advice on how to structure your corporate footprint efficiently - whether that involves a holding company, a free zone entity, an offshore layer or a combination. We work with qualified legal and tax advisors to ensure the recommended structure is appropriate for your jurisdiction, activity and investor requirements.

Banking Introductions

One of the most challenging aspects of establishing a business in the UAE is opening a bank account. We facilitate introductions to relationship managers at UAE-based and international banks, helping clients navigate requirements and improve their chances of a successful account opening for both corporate and personal banking needs.

Business Advisory

Practical, commercial advisory for businesses at various stages - from pre-establishment feasibility through to operational challenges, growth strategy and market entry planning. We draw on direct UAE business experience and our wider professional network to provide grounded, actionable guidance.

International Business Development

For businesses looking to expand beyond their current market, we support the identification of opportunities, the mapping of entry routes and the facilitation of introductions to credible local partners in target markets. We work with an international network of advisors and agents across Europe, Asia, Africa and the GCC.

Strategic Partnerships

Identifying and structuring the right strategic alliances can transform a business. We work with clients to define what they need from a partnership, identify suitable candidates from within our network or through targeted outreach, and support the structuring and documentation of the arrangement.

Sourcing & Trade Facilitation

For businesses involved in trade and physical goods, we assist with supplier identification, sourcing introductions and trade facilitation support. This includes connecting buyers with manufacturers, agents and distributors, and facilitating arrangements that support import and export operations through the UAE.

Referral & Corporate Solutions

For professional service providers and businesses with clients who need UAE-related support, we offer a structured referral programme. We also provide corporate solutions for businesses that require ongoing advisory and introduction support on a retained or project basis.
