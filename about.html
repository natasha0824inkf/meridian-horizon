About Meridian Horizon

A UAE-based commercial brokerage and business advisory firm built on professional relationships and a commitment to long-term client success.

Who We Are

A Trusted Partner for Business in the UAE

Meridian Horizon Commercial Brokers LLC is a UAE-based commercial brokerage and business advisory company. We work with entrepreneurs, SMEs and international investors to navigate the full lifecycle of doing business in the UAE - from initial company formation through to banking, structuring, sourcing and strategic partnerships.

Our approach is advisory-first. Before recommending a structure, an introduction or a partner, we take the time to understand your business, your objectives and your timeline. The result is advice and connections that are genuinely relevant, not generic.

We operate through a trusted network of professional partners - legal, banking, accounting, logistics and regulatory specialists - who we have vetted over time and work with on a regular basis. This allows us to offer end-to-end support without compromising on quality at any stage.
