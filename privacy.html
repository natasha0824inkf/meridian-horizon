2. Data We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • WhatsApp: if you contact us via WhatsApp, your phone number and the content of your messages.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your enquiry and communicating with you about our services.
  • Assessing your requirements and identifying whether and how we can assist you.
  • Providing commercial brokerage and advisory services, including facilitating introductions to professional partners.
  • Maintaining records of our business relationship.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations applicable to us in the UAE.
  • Sending relevant service updates or information where you have indicated you are interested in receiving these - you can opt out at any time.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

For individuals covered by GDPR or UK GDPR, our legal bases for processing personal data are:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): responding to enquiries, maintaining business records and providing advisory services where we have assessed that our interests do not override your rights.
  • Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): where you are a client or prospective client and processing is necessary to provide or discuss our services.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): where we are required to process data to meet regulatory requirements.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): for any optional processing such as marketing communications, where you have given explicit consent.

For individuals in the UAE, processing is carried out on the equivalent grounds under the UAE PDPL.

5. Third Parties

We share personal data with third parties only in the following circumstances:

  • Brevo (Sendinblue): we use Brevo to receive and manage contact form submissions and email communications. Brevo processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. See Brevo's Privacy Policy.
  • Professional partners: where you have asked us to facilitate an introduction or we have identified a relevant professional (legal, banking, advisory), we may share relevant details with that professional to enable the introduction. We do this only with your knowledge and only to the extent necessary.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: where we are required to disclose data by applicable law, court order or regulatory authority in the UAE.

We do not share personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. International Data Transfers

As a UAE-based business with international clients and partners, personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the UAE and the EEA. Where such transfers occur, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Transferring only to countries that provide an adequate level of protection as recognised by applicable law.
  • Using Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent mechanisms where required under GDPR.
  • Sharing only the minimum data necessary for the purpose.

Brevo operates servers in the EU and is subject to EU data protection law. Google Fonts data is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: contact details and correspondence are retained for up to 2 years from the date of last contact, then deleted.
  • Client engagements: records relating to services provided are retained for 7 years from the end of the engagement, in line with UAE commercial record-keeping requirements.
  • Marketing consents: retained until you withdraw consent or opt out.

After the applicable retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Google Fonts loads typeface files from Google's servers when you visit the site. This involves a browser request to Google's CDN which may log your IP address. No cookie is set by this request on our end. If you prefer not to have this request made, you may disable JavaScript or use a browser extension that blocks third-party requests.

We will update this section if we add any additional third-party services (such as analytics) in future.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against accidental loss, unauthorised access, disclosure or destruction. These include:

  • Access controls limiting who within our organisation can access personal data.
  • Using reputable third-party processors (Brevo) who maintain their own security standards.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. If you have concerns about communicating sensitive information through this website, you are welcome to contact us by phone instead.
